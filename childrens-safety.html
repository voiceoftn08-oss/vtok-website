Vtok Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Vtok, operated by Winmoon Technologies, is intended for adults aged 18 and above. Protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation is a priority. Vtok does not permit content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children.

Prohibited content and behavior

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form.
  • Grooming, sexualization, or solicitation of minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, or promoting child sexual abuse material.
  • Using Vtok to threaten, exploit, or endanger a child.

Reporting and blocking

Users should report suspicious or harmful behavior using in-app reporting tools where available. Users may also contact our support email. Block users who make you feel unsafe and do not share harmful content further.

Enforcement

We review safety reports and may remove content, restrict accounts, or report suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities when required by law. Our response depends on the facts and applicable legal requirements.
